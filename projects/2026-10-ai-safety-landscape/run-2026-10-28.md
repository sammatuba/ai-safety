# First collection record

The session date is 2026-10-28 in Asia/Manila. Machine timestamps use Coordinated Universal Time (UTC).
The deliverable is a persistent discovery database and research map. Scientific claim review remains a later pass.

## Method

[INTERPRETATION] The editorial map divides artificial intelligence (AI) safety into 20 areas and 80 subtopics.
The scope includes technical alignment, security, misuse, deployment, governance, human factors, and adjacent ethical questions.
Parent relationships support navigation. They do not assert an accepted classification of the field.

The query plan contains:

- 20 areas multiplied by eight broad coverage facets: 160 queries.
- 20 areas multiplied by two searches restricted to publication platforms: 40 queries.
- 80 subtopics multiplied by paper, resource, and limitation searches: 240 queries.

These 440 standalone searches completed. The first requested five results; later searches requested ten.
The database records 4,395 standalone search hits and ten results from a separate native validation.
Repeated resources merge by canonical URL. Search hits retain the original query, rank, URL, and response snapshot.
Canonicalization merges arXiv version URLs and tracking parameters. It does not merge every cross-platform duplicate.

Retrieval selected resources across parent areas, including their subtopics.
The resource-type heuristic prioritizes publication pages, then repositories, then other pages.
Later selection also uses result rank. This is an editorial selection procedure, not a representative statistical sample.

## Billing and route correction

The standalone Search and Fetch endpoints have a published price of USD 0.75 per 1,000 successful requests [W4].
The server-side promotional note describes a separate billing situation.
The initial inference that a Harness provider's endpoint parity established free eligibility was incorrect.

The first test and the 32-search pilot showed no observed balance change.
After 160 searches, granted balance was USD 0.04 lower. The owner reported no other account usage.
The owner authorized USD 1.00 total for validation and collection.
The final observed granted-balance decrease was USD 0.44. Topped-up balance did not change.
The 582 successful standalone requests imply USD 0.43650 at the published rate.
These observations are consistent with ordinary standalone billing. They are not an itemized invoice.

After the owner clarified the promotion's scope, standalone collection stopped.
The policy now blocks standalone collection even when a paid flag is supplied.
