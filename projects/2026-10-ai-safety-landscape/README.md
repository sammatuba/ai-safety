# AI safety landscape

Build a persistent research database covering artificial intelligence (AI) safety.
Use DeepSeek Search and Fetch to discover resources and preserve provenance.
Generate a knowledge graph and mind map from database records.

[INTERPRETATION] The topic structure is a working navigation scheme. Areas overlap.
It is not a claim that the field has one accepted classification.

See [the collection plan](PLAN.md) and [database instructions](../../literature/landscape/README.md).
The [first collection record](run-2026-10-28.md) reports results, billing correction, validation, and gaps.

The first milestone is a broad collection across every planned area and coverage facet.
Later milestones verify underlying material, reconstruct findings, and compare experiment options.
No milestone treats search-result volume as evidence that the landscape is complete.
