# Quiz 00: map

## Questions
1. Name the five explanations of agreement and the one regime that isolates each.
2. Which lens tells you that two endorsements from one origin are one observation? Which lens tells you why the proposer showed those two items?
3. Where in the review game does the authority transition occur, and which role owns it?

## Answers
1. Independent mistake (A), shared-source error (B), conformity (C), strategic persuasion (D), parallel conduct (E0), collusion (E1, P). Talk without action (F) is a seventh case that is not an explanation of harm but a trap for auditors.
2. Epistemology (document 01: conditional independence, likelihood ratios). Game theory (document 03: the proposer's payoff and selective disclosure).
3. At the decider's action. The decider owns it; the decider is always accuracy-aligned and trusted in the sprint's threat models.
