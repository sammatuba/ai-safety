# Study plan

Tied to `ROADMAP.md`. Each week names the documents to read, the quiz to answer in your own
words before opening the key, the paper to read into a dossier, and one thing to build.
Write notes in `study/notes/YYYY-MM-DD-<slug>.md`. A note has three parts: what I now
understand, what I still cannot explain, one question for a mentor.

## Learning rule

Read a concept document once. Close it. Write the three quiz answers from memory. Open the
key. Fix your answer and write one sentence about what you got wrong and why. Then run the
matching notebook cell and change one number.
