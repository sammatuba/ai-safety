## Worksheet for each paper

Record the paper's question, the agents or participants, and the task.
List the conditions, what changes between them, what remains fixed, and the measured outcomes.
Describe one author-reported result and its limitations, with the section or page.
Then write what the proposed project changes and what the paper may already cover.
Mark anything you have not checked as unverified.

## Compare a paper case with a proposed case

For each idea, sketch one example from its closest paper and one proposed matched example.
Name the independent variable, the outcome, and a plausible alternative explanation.
Explain how a control could separate that explanation from the proposed mechanism.
Use the controls and stop conditions in the review to challenge the sketch.

Ask an expert which close predecessor is missing and whether the comparison isolates a useful mechanism.
Select a project only after feedback. Confirm model access and a budget before model experiments.
