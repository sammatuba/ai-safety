## Idea 1: cancellation across delegated objectives

[INTERPRETATION] This is a narrow extension, not a new interruption or replanning benchmark.

The proposed comparison cancels objective A while preserving objective B.
Existing assigned work, renamed tasks, and retries create opportunities to continue A.
Every model receives the information needed to know that A is canceled.
Otherwise, failure could merely reflect missing delivery of the instruction.

The model proposes actions in a small controlled environment.
The scorer checks intended task membership and permitted actions from a predefined record.
The experiment need not implement a production scheduler or actually concurrent processes.

**Controls:** temporary failure instead of cancellation; single-agent execution; matched context and computation;
permitted cleanup; action timing relative to cancellation.
Do not count an already completed irreversible action as a failure to obey a later instruction.
Do not provide a hard enforcement gate only to the treatment and then call its success improved model behavior.

**Primary candidate measure:** whether any prohibited action pursuing A occurs after the instruction is available.
Also measure permitted completion of B and unnecessary stoppage.
Keep attempted actions separate from executed actions.

**Stop or redesign:** if differences disappear with matched instructions, the delegation claim weakens.
If models already perform at ceiling, pivot to a faithful replication or choose another question.
A null result should limit the claimed failure mechanism rather than trigger unrestricted search for a dramatic example.

## Idea 2: evaluation labels and the next experiment

[INTERPRETATION] This is the easiest small manipulation but has a high risk of yielding generic framing sensitivity.

For each underlying trace, randomize its presentation while preserving the trace and fixed candidate-test menu.
Do not compare different trace families and attribute every difference to labels.
Refusal, absent tools, and external blocking have different evidential implications.

Separate label-only changes from explanatory prose that supplies more reasoning.
Balance length, position, test cost, and budget. Include accurate labels and neutral raw-trace controls.
An inaccurate label tests resistance to misinformation, a different question.
A correct cause label can supply the answer; avoid that leakage.

Score the selected test through independently checkable consequences.
Separate choosing a useful test, interpreting its result, and drawing a supported conclusion.
A direct comprehension probe is a control, not privileged access to a model's reasoning.

**Stop or redesign:** if equivalent prose matches the structured categories, do not claim an ontology-specific benefit.
If only misleading labels produce an effect, reposition the study accordingly.
If the result changes only verbal judgments and not failure discovery, the safety contribution is weaker.

## Idea 3: responsibility for interacting tasks

[INTERPRETATION] This has the strongest connection to management science and energy expertise,
but matched-case construction is harder than the form suggests.

Start from one planning instance with a known hazard.
Change assignment so the relevant dependency belongs to one agent or spans two agents.
Keep facts, permitted communication, and total computation comparable.
Cross that assignment with local duties versus explicit duties to check interactions.

The useful result is a specific interaction between hazard location and assigned safety duty.
A general improvement from longer prompts or more calls does not isolate the mechanism.
All actors can pursue benign objectives. No collusion claim is required.

Include full-information individual comprehension, a single-agent complete-workflow baseline,
and cases where all permitted plans are safe.
Score actual joint plans rather than whether explanations contain safety vocabulary.

**Stop or redesign:** if one agent cannot solve the instance with full information, the task may mainly measure basic capability.
If changing responsibility also changes available evidence, the causal interpretation is confounded.
If a simple explicit shared constraint removes every failure, characterize the resulting limit honestly.

## Publication assessment and next decision

These are [INTERPRETATION], not acceptance predictions.

| Candidate | Best reason to pursue | Main publication risk | First useful artifact |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cancellation | Clear authority boundary and measurable action | Generic instruction-following result | One canceled objective, one preserved objective, and a delegated continuation |
| Evaluation labels | Direct link between representation and experiment choice | Ordinary framing effect without a safety contribution | Identical trace with alternative presentations and two diagnostic choices |
| Responsibility | Established system-safety mechanism under model specialization | Added reasoning difficulty or computation masquerades as responsibility | One fixed hazard under two balanced task assignments |

Read, reconstruct, and obtain expert feedback before choosing.
Then specify a minimum worthwhile effect, uncertainty analysis by task family, and a fixed development budget.
Repeated samples from one case do not replace independent task families.
The hypothesis registry should change only after a concrete project is selected.
